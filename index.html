Biography

Born in a small village in District Swabi, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, I received my early education from the same village. Later on, I got my Bachelor's degree in Geoinformatics Engineering from School of Civil and Environmental Engineering at National University of Sciences and Technology (Pakistan). My undergraduate studies mainly focused on Geoinformatics, and I was mainly trained in Programming, Geographic Information Systems, and GIS Automation. I hold a Master's degree in Remote Sensing and GIS from National University of Sciences and Technology (Pakistan). During Master's I focused on several advanced topics in GIS, Remote Sensing, and Web GIS. After completing my Master's I worked as Lecturer of Geoinformatics in National University of Sciences and Technology, Pakistan where I taught several courses.

I was admitted as a PhD student at Department of Geography and Geospatial Sciences, South Dakota State University, Brookings, South Dakota, USA. I spent two and a half years in the Ph.D program where I studied several courses related to remote sensing, GIS, machine learning, and deep learning . I also worked on UAV remote sensing datasets with machine learning for crop yield and seed protein content estimations . In August 2023, I transferred my Ph.D program to The University of Alabama.

In August 2025, I graduated with a PhD in Geography (Remote Sensing Specialization) from the Department of Geography and the Environment, The University of Alabama, Tuscaloosa, AL, USA. My doctoral research applied machine learning and deep learning to remote sensing data for county-level crop yield prediction in the US Corn Belt.

Since August 2025, I have been an Assistant Professor of Geoinformatics at the National University of Sciences and Technology (NUST), Pakistan. At NUST, I am actively engaged in both teaching and research, with a focus on GIS programming and Geospatial Artificial Intelligence (GeoAI). My teaching centers on equipping students with the computational and analytical skills needed to work with spatial data, while my research explores the integration of machine learning and deep learning with remote sensing and geographic information systems to address real-world environmental and agricultural challenges, including crop yield prediction and other parameters related to food security and policymaking.
